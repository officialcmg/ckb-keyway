Testnet only. Makes a test KeyWay account with your JoyID and EVM wallet as its backups, then you rescue it with each one.

2 Make the test account

3 Rescue it with JoyID

Open the rescue page
  1. Paste the test account address and press Look up.
  2. Press JoyID and approve.
  3. Paste this as the destination:
    —
  4. Press Review. The page shows the text JoyID will ask you to sign, like: 
    KeyWay recovery
Move everything to ckt1…
CKB: …
Tokens: none
Fee: … CKB
Transaction: 0x…
  5. Press Sign and send. Check JoyID shows the same lines.

4 Refill, then rescue it with your EVM wallet

Step 3 moved everything out, so put 1,000 testnet CKB back first.

  1. Open the rescue page again, paste the test account address, press Look up.
  2. Press EVM wallet and approve.
  3. Destination: any testnet CKB address you have (your JoyID one from step 3 works).
  4. Press Review, then Sign and send. Check your wallet shows the same lines as the page.
  5. Tell Claude both transaction hashes, and anything that looked off.
